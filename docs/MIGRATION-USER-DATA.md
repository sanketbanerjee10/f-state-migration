# Data Collected from Applicants

## Applicant profile and game stats

- **In-game name**
- **Current state number**
- **Current alliance name or tag**
- **Kills** — may be left blank or entered as zero
- **Structure Power**
- **Tech Power**
- **Troop Power**
- **Hero Power**
- **Mod Vehicle Power**
- **F1, F2, and F3 arena formation powers** — applicants are asked to exclude season buildings
- **Screenshot** — optional; used to read and prefill game details

Power on Record is calculated from the five power components; the applicant does not enter it separately.

## Migration preferences and explanation

- **Migration type** — Individual or Group
- **Group representative's in-game name** — collected for group applications
- **Preferred destination** — Any alliance or one or more specific alliances
- **Reason for migrating**
- **Additional comments** — optional

Migration tier is calculated from the applicant's power details; it is not entered by the applicant.

## Additional data for group members

For each additional member, the form can collect:

- **In-game name**
- **Current state number**
- **Current alliance name or tag**
- **Kills** — may be left blank or entered as zero
- **Structure, Tech, Troop, Hero, and Mod Vehicle Power**
- **F1, F2, and F3 arena formation powers** — optional
- **Screenshot** — members may be added using screenshots or entered manually

A member's Power on Record is calculated from their five power components. State and alliance can default to the applicant's values. Screenshot-imported entries are marked as verified; manually entered entries are not. The form allows up to 99 additional members.

## Data not entered directly by the applicant

- **Power on Record** and **migration tier** are calculated.
- **Screenshot verification status** is set based on how the data was added.
- **Character reference** is not collected in the current form.
- Screenshot files and raw text extracted from screenshots are used in the browser; they are not included with the submitted form data.
